From July 2021 to January 2022, I worked as a **Designer Engineer** at **Improto 3D** in Medellín, Antioquia.

I led a team of 4 to design, manage, build, and calibrate the largest FDM 3D printer in Medellín. Key responsibilities and outcomes included:
- Supervising all technical aspects and key decision-making to keep the project within schedule and budget constraints.
- Successfully delivering an operational large-scale printer that is currently used to produce sculptures and art for various galleries.
