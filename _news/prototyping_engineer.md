From April 2017 to May 2021, I worked as a **Prototyping Engineer** at **Masmecanica** in Medellín, Antioquia.

I specialized in designing replacement parts for industrial and food processing equipment. Key responsibilities included:
- Designing parts using CNC, SLA, and laser cutting technology.
- Utilizing 3D scanning to create and modify digital copies of industrial parts.
- Programming PLCs for food processing machinery.
