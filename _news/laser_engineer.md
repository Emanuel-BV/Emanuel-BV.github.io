From February 2023 to September 2025, I served as a **Laser Engineer** at **Beta Machine Solutions LLC** in Roselle, NJ.

I acted as a technical representative, providing repair, maintenance, and customization for aesthetic lasers and biomedical equipment across the United States. Key achievements included:
- Partnering with aesthetic equipment sales companies to customize products by analyzing and adapting engineering to new technologies.
- Conducting quality assurance tests, analyzing laser spectra, and performing power/cooling evaluations on various laser systems (Diode, Nd:YAG, Er:YAG, CO2, IPL).
