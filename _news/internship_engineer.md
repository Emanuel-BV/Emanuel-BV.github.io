From December 2019 to July 2020, I completed an **Engineering Internship** at **Grupo Excala - Compañia de Empaques** in Medellín, Antioquia.

I contributed to the company's production processes in the agave and fique industry by:
- Leading the design and development of double-fold sewing equipment for fique sacks in the coffee industry.
- Implementing efficiency improvements to existing production equipment, such as cone winders and spinning machines.
