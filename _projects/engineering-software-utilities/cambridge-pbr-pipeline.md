Between **February and April 2025**, I completed a 3-month engineering and technical documentation contract with **Cambridge Pavingstones with ArmorTec** (New Jersey), serving as the bridge between product engineering, marketing, and architectural specifiers.

The engagement combined **hardware setup, Python/Photoshop data automation, 3D CAD visualization, and customer-facing technical documentation** into a single end-to-end deliverable.

---

## 1. Semi-Automatic 3-Camera Photography Rig & PBR Texture Pipeline

To digitize the full range of pavingstones, wallstones, and color blends for architects and online configurators, manual photography and color correction were too slow and inconsistent.

- **Hardware Capture Setup:** Designed and operated a **semi-automatic 3-camera photography station** capturing **3 to 5 controlled angles per brick** across the full product and color catalog under uniform lighting.
- **Database & Bitmap Generation:** Organized raw captures into a structured product database producing clean **PNG cutouts** and seamless **PBR bitmap textures** suitable for CAD, Blender, and architectural visualization tools.
- **Python + Adobe Photoshop Automation:** Developed automated color-compensation and batch-processing workflows combining **Python scripts and Adobe Photoshop**, standardizing color accuracy across hundreds of SKUs and exporting multiple target file formats ready for marketing's online repository.

## 2. New Product CAD Visualization & Published Bilingual Builders' Manual

In parallel with the digital texture repository, I participated in the product development of the **Pre-Cut & Pre-Packaged Omega Smooth Wall Kitchen Kit (Left)** and partnered with Marketing to create the assembly Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) and customer-facing **Builders' Manual**.

- **3D Assembly & Photorealistic Renders:** Built accurate multi-layer assembly models and produced clean perspective and elevation renders showcasing both smooth white and textured stone finishes.
- **22-Page Bilingual Manual (English & Spanish):** Authored step-by-step construction documentation covering concrete slab/rebar footings, electrical conduit and plumbing waste-line layouts, 7 layer-by-layer block placement blueprints with exact stone BOM tables, lintel preparation, granite countertop tolerances, and stainless-steel appliance installation.
