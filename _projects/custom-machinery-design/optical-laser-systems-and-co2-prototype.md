## 1. High-Power Medical-Aesthetic Laser Systems & Thermal Engineering (Beta Machine Solutions LLC)

Serving as **Optical/Laser & Field Systems Engineer** (Feb 2023 – Present, continuing part-time consulting since Oct 2025) across NJ, NY, FL, and MI, I supported imported clinical platforms for companies including Luna Medical Technology, Supramedical, and Medexx:

- **Optical & Power Diagnostics:** Executed component-level electrical, optical spectra, power-supply, and shot-calibration diagnostics across **Diode (810nm / 755nm), Nd:YAG, Er:YAG, CO2, IPL, RF, and HIFU** systems.
- **Thermal & Hydraulic Cooling Systems:** Repaired and calibrated closed-loop water-cooling circuits (pumps, flow sensors, radiators) and thermoelectric cooling arrays (**Peltier/TEC 2s2p** assemblies, resistance/current verification) critical to protecting laser cavities from thermal drift.
- **Hardware Adaptation (DFMA):** Reverse-engineered and modified electromechanical enclosures and internal mounts in CAD to satisfy US compliance and field-serviceability requirements.

## 2. Embedded CO2 Carboxytherapy Prototype (R&D Hardware Project — In Progress)

To explore open, serviceable biomedical dosing hardware, I am designing a closed-loop **CO2 flow and pressure regulator prototype** integrating embedded software, touch HMI design, and precision pneumatics:

- **Control Architecture:** Built on an **ESP32-S3** paired with a **Waveshare 5-inch capacitive touchscreen (SKU 28151)**, programmed in **C++ (PlatformIO / VS Code)** using **LVGL / SquareLine Studio**.
- **Safety-Critical State Machine:** Implements Finite State Machine (FSM) logic governing **Airtac solenoid valves**, foot-pedal actuation, and live pressure-transducer feedback with automated overpressure shutoff interlocks.
- **Enclosure & Prototyping:** Custom CAD housing engineered in **Autodesk Fusion 360** for rapid iteration via additive manufacturing (Bambu Lab A1).
