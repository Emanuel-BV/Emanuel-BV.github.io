Industrial Reliability, Utilities Energy Audit & Materials Engineering ROI

Quantified $18k–$24k+/year in compressed air energy savings across 3 plants, extended PTFE welding consumable lifespan by +166% (-51% shift cost), and commissioned CAPEX RO/Argon utilities.

Note: Presented in an NDA-safe engineering case study format focusing on empirical methodologies, utility systems, and measurable financial/operational ROI.

1. Multi-Facility Compressed Air Energy Efficiency Audit ($18,200–$24,200+/yr ROI)

  • Problem: Pressure drops on continuous production lines and high energy consumption across 45 kW to 110 kW rotary screw compressors indicated major pneumatic leakage across three manufacturing buildings.
  • Empirical Methodology: Designed a thermodynamic press/depressurization test protocol measuring rise and decay times across the 110–130 PSI band during non-production windows. Combined measured leak percentages with compressor ratings (274.5 to 664.8 CFM), specific motor power (kW/CFM), and facility electric tariffs ($0.05/kWh) in an analytical spreadsheet model.
  • Quantified Findings:
    • Building A: 88.23 CFM lost (32.1% leakage)
    • Building B: 263.76 CFM lost (46.7% leakage)
    • Building C: 147.73 CFM lost (22.2% leakage)
    • Financial Impact: Justified targeted leak remediation identifying $18,204 to $24,272 USD/year in direct annual electricity savings.

2. Tribological & Cost Evaluation of High-Temperature PTFE Welding Tapes (+166% Lifespan)

  • Problem: Premature thermal degradation, tearing, and molten PVC adhesion on 4-point industrial welder heating plates (~550°F) caused frequent mid-shift stops for tape replacement.
  • Experimental Matrix: Benchmarked 8 commercial PTFE/silicone/fiberglass tape references (0.005”–0.0082” thickness) under live production conditions—tracking adhesive peel stability, notched vs. standard frame cycles per change, daily linear consumption (496 inches/day standard), and total cost per shift.
  • Verified Results:
    • Top-performing substrate (Kunststofftechnik Böhme) delivered 923 welded windows per change—a +166% lifespan increase over baseline.
    • Reduced consumable shift cost from $139.63/shift down to $68.40/shift (-51% cost reduction).

3. CAPEX Automated Glass Line Utilities, Electrical Cabinet Audits & PVC ESD Control

  • Process Water & Argon Utilities Commissioning: Supported the CAPEX ramp-up of a high-volume automated insulated glass line (Billco CNC cutting + Erdman Super Spacer assembly, modeled for 600–900 IG units/shift with a 3–4 day Kanban Stay Ahead buffer). Stabilized the KineticoPRO reverse-osmosis (RO) water system, Grundfos SCALA1 3-45 booster pump, multi-stage Pentek BP-420 filtration (50 µm / 5 µm), and 90–95 PSI Argon safety protocols while coordinating directly with OEM engineers.
  • Preventive Electrical Audits & Cross-Plant Training: Audited 14 industrial electrical control cabinets in Building 21 for thermal, wiring, and safety-interlock compliance, documented bilingual light-curtain SOPs, and trained fellow engineers at the Wayne facility to standardize and execute the remaining site audits independently.
  • Electrostatic Discharge (ESD) & Dust Mitigation: Evaluated hybrid static-control strategies on cellular PVC lines—benchmarking active high-frequency AC ionizers (Simco-Ion Phoenix 2.0, Keyence SJ-L005G), conductive carbon-fiber vs. horsehair brushes, and Silvent Air Knives to combat triboelectric debris attraction and surface scratches on dark vinyl.
